About
Rooted in Tradition. Shaped by the Present. Inspired by Nature.

Founded in 2022, the iyal atelier is a multidisciplinary architecture and design studio committed to creating spaces that resonate with timelessness, simplicity, and environmental consciousness. Over the past three years, we’ve grown into a thoughtful practice that harmonizes traditional craftsmanship with contemporary minimalism—offering architectural solutions that are both grounded and forward-thinking.

At the heart of our work is a deep respect for nature and a drive toward sustainability. We believe architecture should not just occupy space—it should engage with it. Our designs integrate seamlessly with their surroundings, embracing natural light, local materials, and passive design strategies that reduce environmental impact while enhancing everyday living.

Our approach is collaborative, detail-oriented, and deeply personal. Whether working on private residences, cultural spaces, or community projects, we strive to reflect the identity of the people who inhabit our designs—while always staying true to our core philosophy: simplicity with depth, sustainability with soul.

As we continue to grow, we remain dedicated to crafting architecture that not only meets the needs of today but also respects the world of tomorrow.
